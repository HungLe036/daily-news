* **Core Facts**:

For global equity and bond markets, the combination of sticky energy inflation and rising 10-year sovereign bond yields severely narrows the runway for central bank policy easing into early 2027.

## 3. Medicine, Psychology & Science

### Reversing 60 Years of Cellular Aging in Human Neurons & Microglial Sleep Reset
* **Impact Score**: `8.8 / 10` (Scope: 9.0 | Permanence: 9.0 | Actionability: 8.5)
* **Sources**: *Aging Cell (University of Bonn), Alzheimer’s & Dementia (University of Kentucky)*
* **Core Facts**:
  - Neuroscientists at the University of Bonn demonstrated a sustained epigenetic age reset: by reprogramming human blood cells from an 80-year-old donor into neural stem cells, they rolled back the cellular molecular age profile to under 20 years without destabilizing genomic integrity.
  - Companion research from the University of Kentucky proved that cognitive deterioration in dementia is heavily mediated by hyperactive microglial immune cells disrupting sleep architecture. Temporarily suppressing these neuroinflammatory cells restored over two hours of restorative deep delta sleep per day in living models, completely independent of amyloid plaque removal.
* **Strategic Implication**:
  - Moves the frontier of neurodegenerative therapeutics from reactive plaque clearance toward proactive neuroinflammation suppression and sleep restoration. Restoring deep-wave sleep provides an immediate, measurable clinical endpoint to arrest cognitive decline in early-stage patients.

### Stress Hormones Permanently Alter Neural Connectivity for Emotional Memory (*Science Advances*)
* **Impact Score**: `7.8 / 10` (Scope: 8.0 | Permanence: 7.5 | Actionability: 8.0)
* **Sources**: *Science Advances (Yale University School of Medicine)*
* **Core Facts**:
  - Yale researchers identified the precise molecular mechanism by which acute spikes in cortisol modify functional communication between the basolateral amygdala and the hippocampus.
  - The study revealed that stress hormones actively bypass standard memory pruning pathways, chemically locking emotionally charged threat perceptions into permanent recall circuits while impairing unrelated factual memory consolidation.
* **Strategic Implication**:
  - Establishes a concrete biochemical target for post-traumatic stress interventions: therapeutic dampening of cortisol-receptor signaling in the immediate hours following acute trauma can prevent the pathological consolidation of intrusive fear memories.
